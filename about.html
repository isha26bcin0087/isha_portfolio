📖 My Story

Hello! I am Isha Sharma, born and brought up in the historic and culturally rich city of Jaipur, Rajasthan. Growing up in the Pink City surrounded by iconic architectural landmarks gave me an instinctive appreciation for how structures are designed and how systems work.

I completed my schooling from Rukmani Birla Modern High School in Jaipur. My school years taught me the power of disciplined study, scientific reasoning, and developing a reflective habit of reading. That curiosity led me to pursue engineering.

Currently, I am in my first year studying Civil Engineering at JECRC University, Jaipur. Alongside engineering mechanics, mathematics, and spatial design, I have developed a specialized focus in Prompt Engineering and working as an AI Generalist. I am fascinated by how artificial intelligence can amplify human creativity and revolutionize traditional engineering domains.
